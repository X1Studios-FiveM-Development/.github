# X1Studios

### Professional FiveM Development

**X1Studios** delivers professional FiveM development focused on **EUP, custom scripts, vehicle liveries, and server assets**.

Whether you are building a new FiveM server or upgrading an existing community, X1Studios provides optimized and immersive content built around your server's identity.

---

## Clothing & EUP

X1Studios offers a wide selection of **HD custom civilian clothing**, including modern and realistic outfits designed to improve immersion while remaining performance-friendly.

We also develop law enforcement EUP for departments including:

* Police
* Sheriff's Offices
* State Agencies
* Federal Agencies

Each pack is designed with clean textures, accurate detailing, and compatibility with standard EUP systems.

### Accessories & Equipment

Custom equipment and accessories are also available, including:

* Hats
* Helmets
* Vests
* Department-specific equipment
* EUP-compatible accessories

## Custom Liveries

X1Studios creates high-resolution vehicle liveries designed for professional FiveM fleets.

Livery development is available for:

* Law Enforcement
* EMS
* Fire Departments
* Civilian Fleets
* Custom Server Departments

Each design is built around your department branding and vehicle fleet.
