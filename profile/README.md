About X1Studios

X1Studios delivers professional development for FiveM, specializing in custom EUP, scripts, liveries, clothing, accessories, and server-focused solutions designed to elevate the roleplay experience.

Whether you're building a new server from the ground up or upgrading an established community, our goal is to provide optimized, immersive, and professionally designed content tailored to your server's vision.

What We Do

👕 Custom EUP & Clothing

We create HD custom clothing designed to improve immersion while remaining performance-friendly.

HD Civilian Clothing Packs — Modern, realistic outfits for civilian roleplay.

Law Enforcement EUP — Custom department clothing for police, sheriff, state, and federal agencies.

Custom Hats & Accessories — Helmets, vests, department-specific gear, and other accessories.

EUP Compatibility — Assets designed to work with EUP-based clothing systems and menus.
